# Massaman Curry
![](https://www.seriouseats.com/thmb/rJ1_vBv8Ln3H1mm8irx3C5_4T8o=/750x0/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc()/__opt__aboutcom__coeus__resources__content_migration__serious_eats__seriouseats.com__recipes__images__2013__01__20130123-238077-dinner-tonight-massaman-curry-eggplant-broccoli-primary-0ed0c5d03bd8488ba99899d2c0f01120.jpg)

Serves: 4

## Ingredients
- 1 can coconut milk (400ml)
- 4tbsp massaman curry paste
- 450g eggplant (cut into 1cm cubes)
- 200g potato (peeled, cut into 1cm cubes)
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 250g broccoli (cut into florets)
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- fresh basil leaves
- steamed jasmin rice

## Instructions
1. Use a spoon to remove a few tbsp of the cream that has risen to the top of the coconut milk can. 
   Heat cream in a wok over medium-high heat until bubbling. 
   Add massaman curry paste, stir constantly, and cook until the fat starts to separate, about 5 minutes. 
2. Add the chopped eggplant and potatoes and stir well to coat in the paste. 
   Cook until eggplant starts to soften, 2 to 3 minutes. 
3. Add sugar, fish sauce, and remaining coconut milk.
   Add enough water to barely cover the vegetables, about 250ml. 
   Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a simmer. 
   Cook until the potatoes are tender, 10 to 15 minutes. 
4. Add broccoli, stir well, and simmer until tender but still crisp, 3 to 5 minutes. 
   Pour in lime juice and season to taste with salt or more fish sauce if desired. 
   Stir in basil and serve curry with rice.
