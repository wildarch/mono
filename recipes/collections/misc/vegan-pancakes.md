# Vegan Pancakes
![](https://svow0ueo.media.greenvideo.io/0c18e53b610964fac175fe0e757adc30557e79b7/2c9288ab94cc935d1c0d282709fafeba0bbc24f2/POSTER_USER/v1/08883e71-c8ce-492a-b20f-3790c1bb29a6.jpg)

Serves: 2

## Ingredients
- 150g self-raising flour (sub for 150g plain flour with 4.5g baking powder)
- 2g baking powder
- .5 tbsp sugar
- .5 tbsp vanilla extract (or use vanilla sugar)
- 200ml plant-based milk
- vegetable oil (for cooking)

## Instructions
1. Whisk the dry ingredients together with a pinch of salt.
   Pour in the milk and mix until smooth.
2. Heat a little of the oil in a non-stick frying pan over a medium-low heat, and add 2 tbsp batter into the pan at a time to make small, round pancakes. 
   You will need to do this in batches of two-three at a time. 
   Cook for 3-4 mins until the edges are set, and bubbles are appearing on the surface. 
   Flip the pancakes over and cook for another 2-3 mins until golden on both sides and cooked through. 
   Keep warm in a low oven while you cook the remaining pancakes.
