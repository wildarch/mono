# Tuna Salad
![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/CfnlQFTmbsY/maxresdefault.jpg)
Simple Tuna salad that is perfect for a tuna melt.

## Ingredients
- 1 can of tuna (water-packed)
- 120g mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 rib of celery (diced very finely)
- 1 or 2 scallions (can be substituted for chives)

## Instructions
In a bowl, combine tuna, mayonnaise.
Mix vigorously with a fork, streaming in olive oil bit by bit to emulsify it.
Fold in the celery, scallions, salt and pepper to taste.
